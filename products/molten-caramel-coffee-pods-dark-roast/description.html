Molten Caramel Coffee Pods bring the full experience of a deep, flavorful dark roast into a fast, no-fuss format. With notes of cocoa, caramelized sugar, each pod delivers a rich cup without the prep time.


Crafted from a blend of Brazilian, Mexican, and Guatemalan beans with Bourbon, Typica, Caturra, Catuaí, Catucaí, and Mundo Novo varietals, this roast strikes a chocolatey and robust taste of an ideal everyday cup. It’s made to shine in single-serve brewers, holding its flavor and depth from the first sip to the last.


It's a great option to offer for those who want the real taste of coffee in less time. No compromise, just quality in every cup.


Ingredients: 100% Arabica

Varietals: Bourbon, Typica, Caturra, Catuaí, Catucaí, Mundo Novo

Acidity level: Low

Manufacturer's country: USA

Regions: Brazil, Mexico, Guatemala

Product amount (oz/lb/g): 12 pods / 4.7oz / 0.31lb / 139g

Gross weight (oz/lb/g): 7.5oz / 0.47lb / 212g

Warning: Do not use if the packaging is damaged or opened. Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture.


Compatibility: Works with most Keurig-compatible machines, including Keurig, Cuisinart, Hamilton Beach, Black & Decker, and more.

Not compatible: Specialty machines such as Nespresso, Ninja, Lavazza, or Dolce Gusto.
