Ceremonial Matcha Powder offers the ultimate matcha experience. Vibrant green, finely milled, and rich in naturally sweet, umami flavor. Made from shade-grown Camellia sinensis tea leaves and stone-ground to a silky powder, this matcha is perfect for daily rituals or mindful moments.


Whether whisked straight with hot water or blended into a creamy latte, it delivers smooth, sustained energy and focus without the crash. It's balanced, gentle energy makes it both a wellness essential and a flavor-forward favorite


Simple. Pure. Elevated.


Ingredients: Matcha tea powder

Varietal: Camellia sinensis (shade-grown green tea leaves)

Grade: Ceremonial

Manufacturer's country: USA

Region: Japan, Kyoto

Product amount (oz/lbs/g): 1.9oz / 0.12lb / 54g

Gross weight (oz/lbs/g): 3.2oz / 0.2lb / 90g

Suggested use: For the traditional experience: Sift 1 scoop (1 teaspoon) of matcha into a bowl. Add 2–3 oz (60–90 ml) of hot water (160–175°F / 70–80°C). Whisk briskly in a zigzag motion until frothy, then enjoy immediately.

For a modern twist: Blend with your favorite milk for a smooth matcha latte.

Warning: Do not use if the packaging is damaged or opened. Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Once opened, consume within 3 months for best quality. If stored properly in an airtight container, in a dry and dark place, it may remain suitable for up to 1 year.
