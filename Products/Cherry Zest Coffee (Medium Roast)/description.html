Cherry Zest Coffee brings a bright, expressive taste that’s equal parts lively and rich. This single-origin medium roast combines citrusy acidity with a deep cocoa foundation, rounded out by notes of black cherry and soft fudge.


Sourced from Guatemala’s high-altitude regions, this coffee highlights Bourbon, Typica, and Caturra varietals. Bourbon provides a sweet, balanced profile with notes of red fruit and chocolate, while Typica adds clean structure and complexity. Caturra delivers bright acidity and a citrusy vibrancy, creating a coffee that’s both dynamic and smooth.


Crafted from beans grown in volcanic soils and washed for clarity, this brew captures the clean, fruit-forward characteristics that Guatemalan coffees are known for. Perfect for pour-over, French press, or drip brewing methods, it’s a flavorful cup with depth that never feels heavy.A spirited cup that zings with brightness, then settles into something deep and satisfying.


Ingredients: 100% Arabica whole beans

Varietals: Bourbon, Typica, Caturra

Acidity level: Medium

Manufacturer's country: USA

Regions: Guatemala, Huehuetenango/Antigua

Product amount (oz/lb/g): 12 oz / 0.8 lb / 340g

Gross weight (oz/lb/g): 13.5 oz / 0.8 lb / 383 g

Warning: Do not use if the packaging is damaged or opened. Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture.
