Your metabolism runs every process in your body - turning food into energy, moving glucose into your cells, and keeping your internal systems in balance. Metabolic Health Formula brings together a thoughtfully selected blend of botanicals to support these everyday processes. At its core is berberine, one of the most researched ingredients for supporting healthy glucose metabolism, paired with olive leaf extract standardized for oleuropein and moringa extract.

This formula is built for adults who are paying attention to their metabolic health - whether you're following a low-carb or lower-sugar way of eating, staying active, or simply wanting daily support as you age. Holy basil, a traditional adaptogen, is included to help support the body's healthy response to ocasional stress, while citrus bioflavonoids and African mango round out a blend designed to work with your body's natural rhythms. Every ingredient is chosen to support metabolic wellness, not override it.

Metabolic Health Formula comes in an easy vegetable capsule with a clean, minimal ingredient list. It's designed to fit seamlessly into a daily wellness routine alongside balanced nutrition and regular activity. For adults who want to take a proactive, plant-forward approach to their metabolic health, this is straightforward daily support you can feel good about.*
