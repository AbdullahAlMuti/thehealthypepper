A perfect day starts with the inviting flavor of Praline Smooth Coffee. This medium roast blend delivers a mellow mix of nutty praline and gentle toffee sweetness, wrapped in a balanced, medium-bodied brew that’s easy to reach for every morning.


Sourced from Brazil, Colombia, and Guatemala, this blend features a carefully selected mix of Caturra, Castillo, Bourbon, Typica, Catuaí, and Mundo Novo varietals. The Brazilian beans provide natural sweetness and body, the Colombian beans add smoothness and clarity, and the Guatemalan beans bring bright, sweet layers of flavor.


Whether brewed with a drip machine, French press, or pour-over, it delivers a flavor that’s both comforting and complex, making it the perfect choice for your daily routine. A dependable, feel-good cup with just the right amount of character.
