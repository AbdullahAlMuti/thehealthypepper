Our Retinol and Peptide Face Serum is crafted to refine and refresh the skin’s appearance overnight. Retinol, known for its smoothing properties, works together with peptides to support the look of skin firmness and texture. The result is a complexion that appears more even and polished over time.


Soothing ingredients like bisabolol and nourishing phospholipids help support a soft, balanced feel, complementing the refining benefits of retinol. Its lightweight, silky texture absorbs quickly without feeling heavy.


Use nightly after cleansing by applying 3–4 drops to the face and neck. Follow with moisturizer, and remember to use sunscreen during the day, as retinol may increase skin sensitivity to sunlight.


Ingredients: Aqua (Water), Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Phospholipids, Retinol, Aluminum Starch Octenylsuccinate, 1,2-Hexanediol, Butylene Glycol, Propanediol, Hexapeptide-11, Ammonium Acryloyldimethyltaurate/Carboxyethyl Acrylate Crosspolymer, Sodium Polyacrylate, Bisabolol.

Manufacturer's country: USA

Product amount (oz/lbs/ml): 1 fl oz / 0.07 lbs / 30 ml

Gross weight (oz/lbs/g): 2.7 oz / 0.17 lbs / 77 g

Suggested use: After cleansing, use fingers to apply 3-4 drops of serum to face and neck at night. Use sunscreen the morning after you apply.

Warning: If pregnant or nursing, consult your physician before use. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If irritation occurs, discontinue use. Shake well before use.
