Snuggle up with a cup of Rich Hazelnut Coffee, a medium roast that brings roasted hazelnut flavor, gentle maple sweetness, and a subtle citrus edge into one comforting brew.


This blend combines Brazilian and Mexican beans, featuring Bourbon, Typica, Catuaí, Catucaí, and Mundo Novo varietals. The Brazilian beans offer rich body and chocolate depth, while the Mexican varietals bring clean structure and bright balance. Together, they create a harmonious blend of nutty richness and sweet, warming flavors with just the right citrusy brightness.


Whether brewed with a drip machine, press, or moka pot, it delivers a flavor that’s both cozy and well-composed. The kind of cup that makes chilly mornings a little easier, and a lot more delicious.
