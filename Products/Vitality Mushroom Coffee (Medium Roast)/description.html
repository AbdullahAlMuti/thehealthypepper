Vitality Mushroom Coffee is where flavor meets function. This full-bodied medium roast combines premium beans from Brazil and Mexico with Lion’s Mane and Chaga mushroom powders, bringing together deep chocolate flavor with the natural benefits of adaptogens.


The blend is made from carefully selected Bourbon, Typica, Catuaí, Catucaí, and Mundo Novo varietals. You get the bold, comforting taste of a traditional cup, balanced with subtle earthy undertones plus support for energy, focus, and vitality. Crafted to help you feel sharp without the crash, it’s ideal for your morning ritual or mid-day lift.


A smarter kind of coffee, made for how you move.
